Fun School Events

🎤

Speech Contests

Children learn to speak on stage without fear. We have English and Urdu speech days to make them confident leaders.

🧠

Quiz Competitions

Fun question games about Science, Islam, and the World. This helps children think fast and learn new facts easily.

🎭

Teblo (Drama Day)

Students act in short plays, wear colorful dresses, and sing national songs. It is a very happy day for parents and kids!

Simple School Rules

✅ Good Things to Do

  • Come to school on time.
  • Wear a clean, proper uniform.
  • Respect teachers and friends.
  • Learn from our painted wall charts.
  • Stay quiet while the teacher talks.

❌ Things Not Allowed

  • No fighting or bad words.
  • No mobile phones in school.
  • Do not break school things.
  • Do not copy in exams.
  • Do not take many days off.

School Calendar

Month What Happens?

Parents Meeting (PTM)

🤝

Why meet teachers? It helps us fix your child's study problems together. We call parents after every big test (May, September, and March) to share the result card. Please bring your child in a clean uniform on PTM day!
